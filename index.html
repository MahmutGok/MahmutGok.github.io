Prompt Benchmark (Task 3)

Reference benchmark: MIHENK (quiz_public_tr.md — 10 items) · Text models evaluated under identical prompt & formatting constraints.

Model Accuracy Time Format Compliance Notes
GPT-OSS 20B (OVH / Pollinations) 8/10 strict (10/10 semantic) 4.86s Full Free · Strict format followed; answered Q2 ("hypothesis") & Q4 ("mitochondria") in English despite Turkish prompt.
Gemini Flash-Lite 10/10 ~3s Full Fastest fully accurate Turkish response; zero extra commentary.
Gemini Flash 10/10 ~8s Full 10/10 exact Turkish answers; strictly respected short-answer constraint.
Gemini Pro 10/10 ~44s Full 10/10 exact Turkish answers; highest latency overhead on straightforward L1–L2 items.
Gemini 4 Neon (Reasoning) 10/10 ~72s Full 10/10 exact Turkish answers; deepest reasoning added 24x latency over Flash-Lite without extra gain on short L1–L2 items.

Multimodal Image & Video Benchmark (16:9)

Fixed prompt: "A first-year computer engineering student studying late at night at a minimalist wooden desk, laptop showing code, glowing digital Pomodoro timer reading '25:00', warm amber desk lamp, rain on dark window, cinematic realism, 16:9."

Video Outputs (16:9 · 3s)

Key Takeaway: On 10 Turkish MIHENK L1–L2 items, every model understood the questions semantically, yet exact-match Turkish evaluation caught a real regression in GPT-OSS 20B (answering Q2 and Q4 in English), while smaller Gemini Flash-Lite matched larger models in 3 seconds. On image generation, prompt adherence diverged sharply on text rendering ("25:00"): only Google Imagen rendered the exact digits, FLUX.1-schnell nailed atmosphere but skipped the timer, SD 3.5 garbled it to "6:0", and NVIDIA SANA lost both laptop and timer. On fast open text-to-video (LTX-Video Distilled), text fidelity and scene motion remained noticeably behind image models.
